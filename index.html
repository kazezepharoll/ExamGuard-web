ExamGuard

Built for exam day

Built at Cavendish University and tested on real lab PCs, so the technology stays out of your way.

01

Never miss an exam

A pop-up and a Windows notification the moment your exam opens, and half an hour before.

02

Saved as you write

Every answer is saved as you go. If your PC restarts, you continue where you were.

03

Fair for everyone

A locked exam room that keeps the exam honest, so your result means what it should.

04

Write properly

Headings, bold, lists, superscripts and a symbol palette (√, π, Σ, Ω) for your essays.

05

Your results, first

Results and your lecturer's comments the moment they are released, term by term.

06

Easy on the eyes

Large, clear questions, and dark, light or your own colours with large text if you need it.

Install

Ready in three steps

  1. 01

    Download

    Press Download for Windows and save ExamGuard-Setup.exe (about 80 MB). Campus Wi-Fi is best.

  2. 02

    Install

    Open the file and follow the steps. ExamGuard is added to your Start menu and desktop.

    If Windows says "Windows protected your PC", choose More info, then Run anyway.
  3. 03

    Activate and sign in

    Choose First time? Activate, type your student number and your lecturer's activation code, then set your own password.

Help

Questions students ask

Still stuck? Ask your lecturer or the Exams Office.

Windows says "Windows protected your PC". Is it safe?

Yes, if you downloaded it from this website. Windows shows this for new programs it has not seen often. Choose More info, check that the file is ExamGuard-Setup.exe, then choose Run anyway. To be extra sure, compare the file's fingerprint (SHA-256) with the one on the release page.

I don't have an activation code

Your lecturer gives each student a one-time code on a printed slip. Ask them, or the Exams Office. You can still sit an exam without it: choose Go straight to the exam sign-in and use the code your invigilator gives you.

I forgot my password

Ask your lecturer or the Exams Office for a new activation code, then choose First time? Activate again and set a new password.

Do I need a camera?

Only for exams that use one; ExamGuard tells you before the exam starts and checks the camera with you. Sit facing a window or a lamp so your face is clear.

Something went wrong during an exam

Stay calm and tell your invigilator. Your answers are saved as you write. If your PC restarts, open ExamGuard and sign in again: the invigilator lets you continue where you were.

How do I update or remove ExamGuard?

To update, download and install the latest version from this website; your account and results are kept. To remove it: Windows Settings → Apps → ExamGuard → Uninstall. Your results stay safe on the university's server.
